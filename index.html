WHY EVERY DROP MATTERS

Water changes everything.

Clean water supports health, learning, and opportunity. This little game is a reminder that water is worth looking after.

✳

Clean water, closer to home

charity: water works to bring clean and safe water to people around the world. When water is close by, communities can spend less time collecting it and more time on the things that matter to them.

↓

Make every drop count

At home, turn off the tap while brushing, fix drips, and run full loads of laundry. Small habits help reduce everyday water waste; they complement—not replace—the work of bringing reliable water to communities.
